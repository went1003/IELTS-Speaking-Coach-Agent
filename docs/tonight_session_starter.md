# Tonight's Session Starter

> First-time setup test only. Future sessions can start by saying: `Start today's IELTS speaking practice.` The coach should reply and begin in English.

Select the `IELTS Speaking Coach` skill and send the following message before starting Voice:

```text
Use the IELTS Speaking Coach skill for a 15-minute Topic Focus session.

Read my private working topic bank at:
data/working_documents/personal_topic_bank/IELTS_Speaking_Complete_A4_Notes_Edition_v2_Working_Copy.docx

Start with the Hometown topic. Begin and continue in English. Ask one question at a time, keep your responses short during practice, and wait until I finish each answer. Do not give a long explanation after every answer.

If I ask in Chinese how to express a word or idea, answer briefly, record it as a Language gap, provide natural English alternatives with short usage differences, and use the best option in my corrected answer and report.

Treat personal material as unverified unless the document or I confirm that it is real. Do not turn model answers or adapted stories into personal facts.

When I say "Please summarize", stop asking questions. Then give me:
1. an approximate IELTS band range;
2. my important grammar and vocabulary problems;
3. minimal corrections;
4. more natural Band 7-level alternatives;
5. reusable collocations and verb phrases;
6. other topics where I can reuse the material;
7. any Language gaps I raised in Chinese;
8. a short spoken recap, an optional continuous repeat review, and a compact Word report.

Begin now with one IELTS Speaking Part 1 question about my hometown.
```

## Spoken Commands

- Start: `Let's begin today's practice.`
- Slow down: `Please ask one shorter question at a time.`
- No interruption: `Please save all corrections until the end.`
- Finish practice: `Please summarize this session now.`
- End: `That's all for today.`
