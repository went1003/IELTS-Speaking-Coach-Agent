# Three-Pass Listening and Shadowing

Shadowing means following the speaker closely. This workflow also includes short pauses for phrase-by-phrase repetition, so call it a listening and shadowing review rather than a turn-by-turn speaking test.

## Prepare once

- Finish corrections first. Prepare the complete corrected answer for every selected question, in original order. Keep the learner's intended facts and substantive content, with natural Band 7-level language, not fabricated claims.
- Use exactly that corrected text in all passes. No new corrections, quizzes, explanations, praise, or confirmation questions inside the sequence.
- Announce the sequence briefly once. Normal pace is the baseline; 1.2x and 0.8x are relative targets, not guaranteed playback settings.

## Pass 1: full answers, about 1.2x

Read each question and its complete corrected answer at a slightly brisk but intelligible pace. Finish the whole selection before pass 2.

## Pass 2: short thought groups, about 0.8x

Break each corrected answer into short natural phrases, usually around 3-7 words when meaning permits. Use clause boundaries, introductory phrases, and natural comma-like breaks; do not merely split at full stops, cut rigidly every N words, or break a verb-preposition or noun phrase unnaturally.

Example: `Afterward, / I reported the discrepancies / to my supervisor / and explained / why the figures were different.`

Speak more slowly and aim for 1-2 seconds of actual silence between groups. A slash in a written script marks a break; do not read the slash or the words "pause two seconds" aloud. Do not wait for a response or assess the learner's repetition.

## Pass 3: complete sentences, normal 1.0x

Reassemble the same text into complete sentences. Read each sentence naturally at normal pace; aim for two seconds of silence before the next sentence. Finish every selected answer, not just the first one.

## Continuity and control

- Learner shadowing, murmuring, or acknowledgements are not requests to stop, restart, correct, or ask another question.
- Respect explicit Stop, 停止, 先停一下, exit, new instructions, and safety-related interruption immediately. Do not interpret quoted occurrences of "stop" inside an answer as a command.
- If Voice interrupts playback before completion, resume from the last known phrase when the channel allows. Do not claim the audio continued when it did not. If position is uncertain, ask one brief clarification rather than skipping material or restarting everything silently.
- Do not keep an endless replay running: complete the requested three passes, then offer full replay, partial replay, or report generation. Stopping playback is not permission to discard the session.

## Platform limitations

These are coaching instructions, not an audio player or an audio-file generator. Voice can approximate faster/slower speech but does not expose exact playback multipliers here. Silence duration and uninterrupted playback depend on the voice client, microphone, network, and turn-taking behavior. Do not promise exact timing or microphone interruption suppression, and do not add an API or recording dependency without permission.

Configure before driving. Never ask the driver to tap, read, or operate the phone in motion; road attention takes priority over completing the drill.
